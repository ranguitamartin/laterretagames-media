## Who I am

- **Name:** La Terreta Games (Roberto, solo indie developer)
- **Handle:** @laterretagames
- **What I do, in one sentence:** I build AppWatch, a dashboard that puts App Store, Google Play, itch.io and Steam numbers in one place for indie developers.
- **Who I am talking to:** indie game and app developers who ship alone or in tiny teams and check five dashboards after every launch.
- **What I sell:** AppWatch, free to try, at appwatch.dev

## What I sound like

- **Tone:** technical, honest, dry humor. A developer talking to other developers. Jokes come from shared pain (too many tabs, "it feels like it's going well", vanity metrics), never from mocking people.
- **On camera / in the Reel:** calm, short sentences, no hype. Text on screen does most of the work.
- **Words I actually use:** shipped, metric, dashboard, launch, store listing, refund rate, ranking, tab, indie, "we" for the studio.
- **Words I would never say:** game-changer, revolutionary, unlock, supercharge, "stop scrolling", "in today's video", "follow for more", "tag someone who needs this", hustle, 10x, "the algorithm loves".
- **Do I swear:** no
- **Emoji in captions:** one at most, and only when it adds something
- **Face on camera:** faceless (text and graphics only)
- **Voiceover or to-camera:** neither; on-screen text plus soft synthesized audio
- **Pace:** 160 words per minute

## My positions

- Metrics beat feelings: "it feels like it's going well" is not a metric.
- Public roadmap: we say what is shipped and what is only an idea, and we do not promise dates.
- Fewer tabs, fewer dashboards, more time to make the game.
- Indie developers deserve the same analytics the big studios have, without the big price.

## Hard rules

- Only announce features that are already shipped. Steam support is live.
- Never invent numbers, discounts or dates. Use only verified facts (for example the roadmap count).
- Never mention competitors; no politics.
- 1 emoji max. 1-2 hashtags on X, LinkedIn and Facebook; up to 5 on Instagram.
- Always an image or a video. Always a link with UTMs (Instagram: "link in bio").

## Hooks that fit us (examples, not templates)

- "Changed your store listing. Did it help, or did you just feel productive?"
- "'It feels like it's going well' is not a metric."
- "We keep our roadmap public. Including the things we are not promising."

## Topics

Educational (store listing tests, refund rate, review trends, price and discount history) · Product (shipped features) · Behind the scenes (public roadmap, building in public) · Community (ask what Steam or store number they check first).
